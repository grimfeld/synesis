# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help.

**Issues are welcome**: bugs, ideas, questions, missing translations. Open one at https://github.com/grimfeld/synesis/issues and say what you did, what you expected and what happened.

**Code is by invitation.** Please do not open a pull request unannounced: open an issue first, and if it is something the app should have, you will be invited to write it. Before your first pull request is merged, you agree to the terms below by adding this line to it:

> I have read CONTRIBUTING.md and agree to its contributor terms.

## Contributor terms

Synesis is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0, and is meant to stay free for everyone, forever (`docs/adr/0021-open-source-under-the-agpl-with-the-name-kept.md`). So that it can, by contributing you agree that:

1. Your contribution is your own work, or you have the right to give it under these terms.
2. You license it under the GNU AGPL-3.0, like the rest of Synesis.
3. You also give the author, grimfeld, a permanent, worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence to use, change and distribute your contribution under other terms too. This lets Synesis be published in app stores whose rules conflict with the AGPL, and lets its licence be changed later if that is ever needed to keep it free.
4. You keep the copyright of your contribution and may use it however you like.

## Before you write code

Read `CLAUDE.md` (the working rules), `docs/PLAN.md` (the design) and `CONTEXT.md` (the words the app uses). Every feature has a Tutorial and an end-to-end test.
