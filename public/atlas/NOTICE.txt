The Map's bundled atlas (ADR 0018), built by scripts/atlas.mjs.

atlas.json — land, lakes and rivers from Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com),
public domain, via github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector; clipped to the
Bible's world and simplified.

relief/ — hillshade computed from Mapzen's Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data
(registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles). Terrain Tiles combine, for this region:
SRTM (NASA, public domain), GMTED2010 (USGS, public domain) and ETOPO1
(NOAA, public domain: Amante, C. and B. W. Eakins, 2009. ETOPO1 1 Arc-Minute
Global Relief Model. NOAA Technical Memorandum NESDIS NGDC-24). Attribution
as the Terrain Tiles project asks: "Mapzen Terrain Tiles".
