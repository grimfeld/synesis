# Timeline

The Timeline lays everything dated in your Vault along the Bible's chronology, so you can see what happened when, and who was alive at the time. Your Events share the top Lane; each dated Subject, such as a Character with a Born and a Died Date, has a Lane of its own.

![The Timeline: the Events Lane on top, one Lane per dated Subject below](image:timeline)

1. Open [Go to Timeline](command:nav.timeline). The **Events** Lane is on top, then one Lane per dated Subject, earliest first. A pair of Dates, such as Born and Died, draws a bar; an approximate Date, such as "c. 1513 BCE", is drawn faded.
2. Zoom with the mouse wheel or a pinch, from millennia down to a single year, and drag to move along; **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit all** in the header do the same. Dates too close together to draw apart become a Cluster, a mark with a number: hover it to list them, or click it to zoom until they separate.
3. Narrow what you see: type a title in **Search Lanes**, or open **Filter** to choose Kinds, Dates (such as Born) and Tags. **Only Lanes in view**, on by default, hides the Lanes with nothing in the years on screen. **Clear filters** brings everything back.
4. Click a mark to open its Hub. A dated Subject's Hub shows its own Lane in miniature, with **Open in Timeline** to bring you back here, narrowed to it.
5. Try it once: create an Event you are studying with [New Event](command:create.event) (or the **Event** button in the Timeline's header), and give it a Start Date such as "c. 58 CE". Back on the Timeline, search for its title: it sits on the Events Lane. If it was only a test, the delete button in its Hub's header removes it.

## Good to know

- Write a Date the way you would say it: "c. 1513 BCE", "14 Nisan 33 CE", "52 CE". A Date the app cannot read stays off the Timeline, and its Hub says so.
- An Event is also drawn on the Lane of each Character and Place named in its Characters and Place Properties.
- Kinds and **Only Lanes in view** are remembered; the search, Tags and Dates filters last until you close the app.
