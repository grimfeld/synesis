# Pairing

Pairing makes two of your Devices trust each other for one Vault, so they sync directly with no account and no sync service. You do it once per Device: this Device shows an Invite, the other one uses it, and you approve it here.

1. [Go to Settings](command:nav.settings). Under **Sync** → **Pair your devices**, press **Show pairing code**. A QR code and a line of text appear: this Vault's Invite. It stays valid until you press **Revoke and make a new code**, and no Device joins without your approval.
2. Look at the list under the code: every Device that holds this Vault, this one marked *(this device)*. A green dot means online. The bin button beside another Device removes it from the Vault.
3. Try it once: on your phone or another computer, install Synesis and open it. Press **Get started**, then **Pair with another device**; scan the QR code with **Scan**, or paste the text, and press **Join from another device**.

   Back on this Device, **Allow** the request that appears. The other Device receives the Vault and shows up in the list here. From now on the two sync directly whenever both are online: write a line on one and watch it arrive on the other.

## Good to know

- Anyone with the code can ask to join, so keep it private; revoking makes a new one and the old one stops working.
- **Keep syncing in the background** lets this Device keep syncing after you close its window.
- On a Device that already has a Vault open, press **Close this vault** in Settings first. The start screen then lists **Your vaults**; press **New vault** to reach **Pair with another device**.
