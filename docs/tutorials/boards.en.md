# Boards

A Board is where you think a talk out before you write it: your own bubbles, material from your Vault and labelled groups, laid out by hand. Every Composition can have one.

![A Board with bubbles, cards and a labelled group](image:board)

1. Open a Composition you are preparing, or create one with [New Composition](command:create.composition), then switch to its Board with [Board](command:doc.board). The **Board** tab at the top of the page does the same, and **Talk** brings the text back.
2. Press **Add note** in the Board's toolbar, type a thought in your own words, and click the empty Board to finish. Drag the bubble where it belongs; double-click it to edit it. To remove anything from the Board, click it and press Delete.
3. The **Material** list on the right holds the Notes and Clippings that share Tags or Passages with this Composition. Click one to place it as a card showing its opening words, or type in **Search the Vault** to find anything else. A card on the Board commits nothing: the talk has not used it until its text Mentions it.
4. Press **Add group** for a section of the talk, and double-click the group's label to name it. Drag the cards for that point inside it. To connect two cards, click one and drag the dot on its right edge onto the other.
5. Try it once: sketch the talk you are preparing, with a bubble for each main point and the material for it beside it. Then switch **Arranging** to **Reading** and click a card: its document opens.

## Good to know

- The Board is saved beside the Composition as a `.canvas` file, which Obsidian opens as a canvas of its own.
- A Version of the Composition covers its Board too, and restoring one restores both.
- **Fit to Board** shows everything at once; **Export the Board** saves it as a picture (PNG or SVG).
- On a phone you can pan, zoom, add notes and long-press a card to move it; groups, connections and the Material list need a larger screen.
