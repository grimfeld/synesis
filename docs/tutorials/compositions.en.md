# Compositions & Embeds

A Composition is a talk or a piece you write for others, by hand and from scratch. Your Notes are there to consult while you write, never to be copied in. A Clipping can be embedded instead, so a quotation and its Source travel together and always show the Clipping as it is now.

![A Clipping embedded in a Composition, shown live with its Source](image:embeds)

1. Create one with [New Composition](command:create.composition): give it a **Title** and press **Create**. It opens in the editor and is listed on [Home](command:nav.home) under **Compositions in progress**. To remove it, use the **Delete** button in its header.
2. Write your opening in your own words. To consult a Note, open it with [Search](command:nav.search), read it, and come back with [Back](command:nav.back): nothing is copied across.
3. Try it once: on a new line of your Composition, run [Insert embed](command:editor.embed), type a few words of a Clipping's Source and pick the Clipping from the list. The quotation appears below the line, with where it came from. If you have no Clipping yet, keep one first: [Tutorial: Clippings](command:tutorial.clippings).

## Good to know

- An Embed is written `![[…]]` in the file, the way Obsidian writes one, so the vault stays readable there.
- Edit the Clipping and every Composition that embeds it shows the new words.
