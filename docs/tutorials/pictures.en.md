# Pictures

A map, a scanned page or a photo of a site can sit in any Note, Composition, Clipping or About. Drop it, paste it or pick it: the picture is copied into your vault's `Attachments` folder and shown where you put it.

1. Open one of your Notes with [Search](command:nav.search). Drag a picture file from your computer onto the text. It appears on a line of its own where you dropped it.
2. Click the picture. Its line shows what is stored, `![[Attachments/…]]`, named after the Note. Move the cursor to another line and the picture comes back.
3. Try it once: create a Note with [New Note](command:create.note) (its **Delete** button in the header removes it again). Run [Insert picture](command:editor.picture) and choose a picture: it appears at the cursor.

## Good to know

- Pasting a screenshot works too. Only jpg, png, gif and webp pictures can go in text; other files are refused.
- A Hub's About takes pictures the same way, even before you have written anything in it: drop one on its empty box.
- Pictures are stored at most 1600 pixels wide, so the vault and sync stay light. An animated gif is kept as it is.
- Source mode shows the text only, `![[…]]` included. Obsidian shows the same pictures.
- Removing the line removes the picture from the text, not from `Attachments`, so undo always brings it back.
- A Paired Device receives the picture with the text.
