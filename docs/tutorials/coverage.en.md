# Coverage

Coverage shows how much you have written about each part of Scripture: one square per Chapter, shaded by how many of your documents mention it. Use it to see where your study has gone deep and which Books you have not touched yet.

1. Read the grid: one row per Book, the Hebrew-Aramaic Scriptures first, then the Christian Greek Scriptures. A grey square is a Chapter nothing mentions yet; the darker the square, the more documents mention it. The count in the header is how many Chapters you have written about, out of all 1,189.
2. Point at a square to see its Chapter and how many documents mention it. Click a Book's name to open that Book's page, where its Chapters are shaded the same way, then come back with [Go to Coverage](command:nav.coverage).
3. Try it once: find the darkest square, the Chapter you have written most about, and click it. Its page opens: the Verses are shaded the same way, and a Verse opens the documents that mention it.

   Every square still grey? Write a Note with [New Note](command:create.note) that names a Passage, such as Romans 8:28, and come back: its Chapter is now shaded. Remove the Note later with the delete button in its header.

## Good to know

- A document counts once per Chapter however often it mentions it, and every kind of document counts: Notes, Clippings, Compositions, Subjects.
- A Passage that spans several Chapters shades each of them.
