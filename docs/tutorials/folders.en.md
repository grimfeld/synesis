# Folders

Folders arrange your Notes and Compositions in the sidebar. A Folder is an ordinary folder inside `Notes` or `Compositions` in your Vault, so one made in Finder, Explorer or Obsidian shows up here too. Folders only arrange: what a document is about is still said by its Tags and Mentions.

1. Open the sidebar and look at the **Notes** and **Compositions** groups. Folders come first, then documents, each in natural order ("Week 2" before "Week 10"). The number on a Folder's row counts every document inside it, nested ones included.
2. Click a Folder's row to unfold it, and again to fold it. Folders start folded, and the app remembers on this Device which ones you opened.
3. Try it once: [Search](command:nav.search) for a Note that sits in a Folder and open it. The Folders holding it unfold on their own, so you can see where it is. Now fold the Folder that holds it, and unfold it again. (No Folder yet? Make one inside `Notes` in Finder, Explorer or Obsidian, put a Note in it, and it appears here within seconds.)

## Good to know

- A Folder made, renamed or removed outside the app shows changed in the sidebar within a few seconds.
- A Note or Composition stored outside its own folder is listed under its real path from the top of the Vault, marked with a leading `/`: `/Study` for a folder of your own, `/` for the top of the Vault itself.
- Only Notes and Compositions have Folders. Sources stand on the Library's Shelves; Places, Characters, Concepts and Events stay in their own groups.
- Hover over a Folder to see its full path in the Vault.
