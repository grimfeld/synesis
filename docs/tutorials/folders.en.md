# Folders

Folders arrange your Notes and Compositions in the sidebar. A Folder is an ordinary folder inside `Notes` or `Compositions` in your Vault, so one made in Finder, Explorer or Obsidian shows up here too. Folders only arrange: what a document is about is still said by its Tags and Mentions.

1. Open the sidebar and look at the **Notes** and **Compositions** groups. Folders come first, then documents, each in natural order ("Week 2" before "Week 10"). The number on a Folder's row counts every document inside it, nested ones included.
2. Click a Folder's row to unfold it, and again to fold it. Folders start folded, and the app remembers on this Device which ones you opened. Open a Note that sits in a Folder, with [Search](command:nav.search), and the Folders holding it unfold on their own.
3. Try it once: beside **Notes**, open the **…** menu and choose **New folder**. Type a name, say "Sermons 2026", and press **Create**: the Folder appears with 0 beside it, and in your Vault it is the folder `Notes/Sermons 2026`. Now put a Note in it: open the **…** beside a Note and choose [Move to folder…](command:doc.move_to_folder), then pick "Sermons 2026". On a computer you can also drag the Note onto the Folder. The Folder now counts 1.

## Good to know

- A Folder made, renamed or removed outside the app shows changed in the sidebar within a few seconds.
- A Note or Composition stored outside its own folder is listed under its real path from the top of the Vault, marked with a leading `/`: `/Study` for a folder of your own, `/` for the top of the Vault itself.
- Only Notes and Compositions have Folders. Sources stand on the Library's Shelves; Places, Characters, Concepts and Events stay in their own groups.
- Hover over a Folder to see its full path in the Vault. On a computer, right-click a Folder for its menu.
- A Folder's **…** menu also makes a Folder inside it. A name cannot be empty, start with a dot, contain `/ \ : * ? " < > |`, or repeat a name already beside it (whatever the capitals).
- A Folder's **+** writes a new Note straight inside it. A Note keeps its title when it moves; a Folder that already holds a file of the same name refuses it rather than add a number. A Composition's Board moves with it.
- Only an empty Folder can be deleted: tidying never deletes a document. Files your computer leaves behind on its own (`.DS_Store`, `Thumbs.db`) do not count.
- [New Note](command:create.note), Home's quick capture and the Command Palette always put a new Note at the top of `Notes`; only a Folder's **+** puts it inside.
