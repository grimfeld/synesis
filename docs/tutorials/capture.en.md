# Quick capture & Notes

A Note is your own thinking, usually about a passage you are reading. Quick capture gets a thought down before it slips away, without deciding on a title or a place for it first.

1. Run [Go to Home](command:nav.home). The **Quick capture** box at the top takes a thought as it comes: **Create** turns it into a Note titled with today's date and time, and leaves you on Home for the next one.
2. Away from Home, [Quick capture](command:create.quick) opens the same box over whatever you are reading. There, **Create** also opens the new Note so you can keep writing.
3. Find your Notes in the sidebar: the **Documents** tab lists every one under **Notes**. The ones you touched last also lead the **Recent** list on Home.
4. When a thought already has a name, use [New Note](command:create.note) instead and fill in its **Title**. Left empty, the title is the date, as with a capture.
5. Try it once: in the **Quick capture** box on Home, write one thought from today's reading and press **Create**. Open it from **Recent** to carry on writing; to remove it, use the delete button in its header.

## Good to know

- A Scripture reference in a capture, such as `Ro 12:2`, becomes a Passage like anywhere else.
- A dated title can be changed later: click the title at the top of the Note and type a new one.
- On a Source's page, the same kind of box keeps a Clipping or a Note from that Source instead.
