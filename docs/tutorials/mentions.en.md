# Mentions & Tags

A Mention links what you are writing to a Subject, a Source or any other document. Write it inside a sentence when the name belongs there, or as a Tag when it matters but would read badly in the prose: both are the same link.

1. Open one of your Notes. A name written as `[[Paul]]` shows as a link inside its sentence; the chips under the title are the Note's Tags. Click either kind: it opens the page it names.
2. On that page, **Backlinks** lists your Note once, however many times it mentions the page. A Tag is the same link as one in a sentence; its row is only marked `#`.
3. Create a Note to practise in with [New Note](command:create.note). Write a sentence and, where a name belongs, run [Insert link](command:editor.link): it types `[[` and lists your documents as you type. Pick one and the name becomes a link.
4. Try it once: under the title, click **Add tag** and name a Subject your sentence leaves out. Open the tagged page and the linked one: your Note is under **Backlinks** on both. Keep the Note, or delete it with the delete button in its header.

## Good to know

- A Tag can also go in the text: [Insert tag](command:editor.tag) types `#` and lists your Tags and pages.
- Any Tag names a document. When there is no page by that name yet, clicking the Tag offers **Create page**.
- A Tag of several words is written with hyphens: `#second-journey`.
