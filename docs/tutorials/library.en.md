# Sources & the Library

A Source is someone else's work that you study: a book, a periodical, an article, a video, a talk. The Library shows every Source you hold on Shelves by kind, so you can see at a glance what you have and open any of them.

![Sources on Shelves by kind, each with a Cover](image:library)

1. Open the [Library](command:nav.library). Each Shelf holds the Sources of one kind, and each card shows the Source's Cover: the picture you gave it, or one drawn from its title, kind and date.
2. Click a card to open the Source's page (if the Library is still empty, the next step adds a Source). A chapter of a book or an issue of a periodical is a Source too, but it sits inside its parent: it is listed under **Contains** on the parent's page and never gets a Shelf of its own.
3. Try it once: add something you are reading with [New Source](command:create.source). Give it a **Title**, pick its **Kind**, optionally a **Cover**, leave **Part of** empty and press **Create**. On its page, type a chapter title in the box under **Contains** and press Enter. Back in the [Library](command:nav.library), your Source stands on its Shelf and its card counts one part. To remove them, use the **Delete** button in each page's header.

## Good to know

- A Source whose kind the app does not know, or a chapter whose parent is missing, waits on the **Unshelved** row until you fix its **Kind** or **Part of**.
- A parent's page gathers everything cited from the Sources inside it.
