# Candidates

A Candidate is a document in your Vault, most often a Note or a Clipping, that shares a Tag or a Passage with a Composition but that the Composition does not Mention yet: material you might still pull in. They are gathered beside the Composition while you write, so nothing relevant is forgotten.

1. Open one of your Compositions from **Compositions in progress** on [Home](command:nav.home), where each card counts its Candidates, or create one with [New Composition](command:create.composition) (its **Delete** button in the header removes it). If it has no Tag or Passage yet, add a Tag at the end with [Insert tag](command:editor.tag).
2. Look at **Candidate material** in the right panel (**Toggle side panel** in the header shows it). Each Candidate lists the Tags and Passages it shares with the Composition. Click one to read it, and come back with [Back](command:nav.back).
3. Try it once: pull a Candidate in. On a new line of your Composition, run [Insert link](command:editor.link) and type its title, or embed a Clipping with [Insert embed](command:editor.embed). It leaves **Candidate material** and moves to **Used in this Composition**.

## Good to know

- A Tag naming the Candidate counts as a Mention too.
- Placing a Candidate on the Composition's **Board** does not use it: it is listed under **On the Board** and stays a Candidate until the Composition itself Mentions it.
