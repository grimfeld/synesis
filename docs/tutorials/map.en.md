# Map & Journeys

The Map shows every Place in your Vault that has coordinates, on an atlas of the Bible's world, and draws your Journeys as routes through their Stops. Use it to see where the things you study happened, what kind of places they were, and which Places a Book or a journey takes you through.

![The Map: Places as pins shaped by their kind, on the atlas](image:map)

1. Open [Go to Map](command:nav.map). Each Place with coordinates is a pin whose shape says what kind of place it is: a settlement, a mountain, water or a site; a region is shown by its name alone. The **Legend** in the corner lists the kinds on screen. Click a pin to open its Hub.
2. Narrow the Places: type in **Search Places**, or open **Filter** to choose Tags, the Books a Place is **Mentioned in**, or **Only Places something mentions**. **Clear filters** brings every Place back.
3. In **Filter**, **Colour by** chooses what the pins' colour tells you: **Kind** (the default), **Tag**, **Book** or **None**. With Tag or Book, pick up to five to compare: each gets a colour, a Place in two of them gets a split pin, and every other Place turns grey. The ✕ beside a value in the **Legend** drops it from the comparison.
4. In **Filter**, turn on a Journey under **Journeys**: its route is drawn in travel order, in its own colour, with each Stop numbered on the edge of its pin and arrows for the direction. A Place visited twice carries both numbers. On a Journey's Hub, **Show on map** does the same, and the **Stops** list says which Stops cannot be drawn.
5. A Place's kind is chosen on its Hub under **Kind**: one of the five that come with the app, or **New kind…** to make your own from a name and an icon. A Place picked from the list of Bible places gets its kind with its coordinates; for Places you made before, [Fill in Place kinds from the gazetteer](command:map.fill_kinds) names each one it would change and asks first.
6. A Place without coordinates is not on the Map. Open its Hub and press **Set location**: pick the place from the list of Bible places that comes with the app, or click the map, then press **Use this location**.
7. Try it once: create a Journey you are studying with [New Journey](command:create.journey). On its Hub, press **Add a Stop** and type the first Place: your own Places are offered first, then Bible places, which become Places of yours, coordinates and kind included, when you pick one. Add each Stop in turn, fix the order with the arrows, then press **Show on map**: the route is drawn through your Stops. If it was only a test, the delete button in its Hub's header removes the Journey; Places it created stay, and are removed the same way.

## Good to know

- A Place's kind is its **Kind** Property, plain text that Obsidian shows as it is. Kinds you make are kept in the Vault, so your other devices draw the same pins; deleting one in Settings leaves your Places as they are, with the plain pin.
- A Journey's Stops are the links in its **Places** Property, in travel order, so Obsidian shows and edits the same list.
- **Colour by**, the Books filter and **Only Places something mentions** are remembered; the search, Tags, the values you compare and Journeys last until you close the app.
- The atlas comes with the app and works offline, down to the scale of a region. Closer in, a street map from OpenStreetMap takes over when you are online; your pins and routes never need a connection.
