# Map & Journeys

The Map shows every Place in your Vault that has coordinates, and draws your Journeys as routes through their Stops. Use it to see where the things you study happened, and which Places a Book or a journey takes you through.

![The Map: Place markers, and a Journey drawn as a numbered route](image:map)

1. Open [Go to Map](command:nav.map). Each Place with coordinates is a marker labelled with its title; click one to open its Hub.
2. Narrow the Places: type in **Search Places**, or open **Filter** to choose Tags, the Books a Place is **Mentioned in**, or **Only Places something mentions**. **Clear filters** brings every Place back.
3. In **Filter**, turn on a Journey under **Journeys**: its route is drawn in travel order, with each Stop numbered and arrows for the direction. A Place visited twice carries both numbers. On a Journey's Hub, **Show on map** does the same, and the **Stops** list says which Stops cannot be drawn.
4. A Place without coordinates is not on the Map. Open its Hub and press **Set location**: pick the place from the list of Bible places that comes with the app, or click the map, then press **Use this location**.
5. Try it once: create a Place you are studying with [New Place](command:create.place), type its name in **Look up a Bible place** and pick it to fill its coordinates, then press **Create**. Back on the Map, search for it: its marker is there. If it was only a test, the delete button in its Hub's header removes it.

## Good to know

- To make a Journey, create it with [New Journey](command:create.journey) and list its Stops in order in its **Places** Property, as links: `[[Antioch]], [[Corinth]], [[Antioch]]`. Its Hub's **Stops** list reorders and removes them.
- The Books filter and **Only Places something mentions** are remembered; the search, Tags and Journeys last until you close the app.
- The map background comes from OpenStreetMap and needs a connection; your markers and routes do not.
