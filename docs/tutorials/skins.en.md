# Skins

A Skin is the app's look: its colours and fonts, with a light side and a dark side. The Skin belongs to the Vault, so every Device that opens it wears the same one; whether you see its light or its dark side, and how large the text is, is each Device's own choice.

1. [Go to Settings](command:nav.settings) and find **Appearance**. **Mode** picks Light, Dark, or whatever the system uses; **Text size on this device** scales every text in the app. Neither changes what your other Devices show.
2. Open the **Skin** list and pick another one: the whole app changes at once, on every Device that shares this Vault. Synesis, Sepia and High contrast come with the app.
3. [Browse Skin gallery](command:skins.gallery): Skins other people made and shared. Tap one to try it on; the app wears it while the gallery is open, and **Light** and **Dark** at the top show each side. Closing the gallery takes it off again.
4. To make a Skin your own, pick it and press **Customize** (or **Duplicate** on a Skin that is already yours). Change the three colours, the fonts and the spacing below; every change shows at once and is saved after a moment. **Undo changes** goes back to how it was when you opened the editor.
5. **Export…** saves the Skin as a file you can send to someone; **Import…** brings in a Skin file you were given.
6. Try it once: open the [Skin gallery](command:skins.gallery), tap **Paper** to try it on, and press **Install**. This adds Paper to your Vault and makes it the one you wear. To go back, pick your old Skin in the **Skin** list; to remove Paper, pick it and press **Delete**.

## Good to know

- Skins live in your Vault, in `.bible-study/skins/`, and reach your other Devices with everything else. Obsidian ignores them.
- An installed Skin is yours: editing it doesn't change the gallery, and a newer version in the gallery doesn't change it. Install it again to get the newer one.
- The gallery only lists Skins accepted into the Synesis repository, and needs a connection while it is open. To add one of yours, see `skins/README.md` in the repository.
