# The Delivery view

The Delivery view is where you give a talk from: the Composition fills the screen, with its text, its Board or both, and a timer. Nothing in it can be edited and nothing in it leads away, so a tap mid-talk never loses your place. The screen stays awake while it is open.

This Tutorial is hidden while the view is open: read a step, do it, then press **Exit** to come back here.

1. Open the Composition you are preparing with [Search](command:nav.search), then run [Deliver this Composition](command:doc.deliver); the **Deliver** button in its header does the same. The talk fills the screen from its beginning, every line shown without its markdown. Press **Exit**, or Escape, to come back to the page as you left it.
2. Deliver again and tap a Passage or a link: it opens in a card over the talk, never away from it. Escape or a tap elsewhere closes the card. **Show** in the bar switches between **Talk**, **Split** and **Board**, and **Larger text** and **Smaller text** size the talk (pinch on a touch screen). Then Exit.
3. Deliver again and press **Start**. The timer counts down from the Composition's `duration`, turns amber for the last two minutes and red at zero, then counts the time over as `+1:30`. **Pause** stops it and **Reset** sets it back. Exit and Deliver again: the timer kept running.
4. Try it once: rehearse the opening of your talk. Deliver it, press **Start**, read your first section aloud, and Exit. Nothing in your Vault changes.

## Good to know

- The timer counts down from a `duration` Property, a number of minutes, set on the Composition while you prepare. Without one it counts up from 0:00.
- The view opens on the face that was showing: Talk, Split or Board.
- The text size is kept on this Device; the Board keeps its own zoom.
- The app's shortcuts and the Command Palette are off while the view is open, so nothing runs behind it.
- Where the screen cannot be kept awake, the bar says "This screen may go to sleep".
