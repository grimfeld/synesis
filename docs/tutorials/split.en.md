# Talk and Board side by side

Writing a talk while looking at its Board needs no flipping between tabs: Split puts the talk on the left and its Board on the right, both working. Only a Composition and its own Board split this way.

1. Open the Composition you are preparing with [Search](command:nav.search), then run [Split talk and Board](command:doc.split). The talk sits on the left and its Board on the right. The **Split** tab, between **Talk** and **Board** at the top of the page, does the same.
2. Drag the divider between the two sides to give one of them more room. Click the divider and the arrow keys move it too. Each side keeps at least 320 pixels.
3. Click a card on the Board, then click into the talk. The card stays selected, but the keys now go to the talk: Backspace there deletes text, never the card. The side you are working in shows a line along its top edge, and the Board's keys work again once you click it.
4. Beside the talk, the Board's **Material** list starts closed to leave the Board room: open it from the arrow on the Board's right edge. With the side panel open, its **Candidate material** follows the Board as you place cards, moving them under **On the Board**.
5. Try it once: with your talk split, press **Add note** on the Board and write down the next point you want to make, then click the empty Board to finish. It sits beside your text until the talk uses it; click it and press Delete to remove it.

## Good to know

- Split needs room for both sides. On a phone, or in a window narrowed by the side panel and this Tutorial, the **Split** tab is hidden and the talk shows; widen the window or close the side panel and the split comes back.
- Run [Split talk and Board](command:doc.split) again, or press **Talk**, for the text alone.
- In **Reading**, a card opens its document; Back returns to the Composition, still split.
- The divider's place lasts until you close the app.
