# Hubs & Backlinks

A Hub is the page of a Source or a Subject: a Book, Chapter or Verse, a Place, Character, Concept, Event or Journey. You do not write it so much as read it: it gathers everything in your Vault that points at it.

1. Open a Hub: click a link to a Subject in one of your Notes, find one by name with [Search](command:nav.search), or open a Verse with [Open a Passage…](command:nav.passage). The header says what kind of page it is; below it comes what belongs to that kind, such as a Place's map, a Source's Clippings or a Character's Dates.
2. Scroll to **Backlinks**: every document that Mentions this page, one row per document however many times it does. On a Subject's Hub they are grouped by Book, in Bible order. A row that Mentions it more than once says how many times; its arrow, **Show every mention**, lists each one.
3. Click a Backlink to open that document, then come back with [Back](command:nav.back).
4. Try it once: open the Hub of a Subject or Source you study and, under **About** at the bottom, click the prompt and write a line in your own words: why it matters to you, or where to start reading. It is saved in the Hub's own file, and Passages and links in it work as in any Note. If the About already has text, add yours after it.

## Good to know

- A Tag and a link inside a sentence are the same Mention, so both make a Backlink.
- **Unlinked mentions**, beside Backlinks, lists documents that write this page's name without linking it.
- A Place, Character, Concept, Event or Journey may go by other names: add them under **Aliases** in its header, such as Saul for Paul or Joppa for Jaffa. A link to an alias opens this Hub, and an alias written without a link counts as an Unlinked mention. A Place's modern name is not an alias.
- A Hub lists its Boards too: the Compositions whose Board holds a card for it.
