gazetteer.tsv is derived from the OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data
(https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data), by Stephen Smith,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

For each ancient place the highest-scoring modern identification was kept:
its coordinates and modern name. Columns: name, latitude, longitude,
modern name, number of verses mentioning the place, OpenBible slug, score.
