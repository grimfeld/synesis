# The Synesis name and icon

The code of Synesis is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE`). The name **Synesis** and its logo and icon (`branding/`) are not covered by that licence: they belong to the author, grimfeld, and say "this is the app made here".

You may:

- build Synesis from this repository, unchanged, and share it under its own name and icon;
- say that your work is "based on Synesis" or "a fork of Synesis";
- use the name and logo to write about Synesis or to link to it.

You may not, without written permission:

- give a changed version the name Synesis, or a name that could be mistaken for it;
- show the Synesis logo or icon on a changed version, its store listing or its website;
- suggest that your version, service or organisation is made, endorsed or supported by Synesis.

If you fork Synesis, pick a new name and a new icon. The AGPL still applies to your code: share the source of what you change with everyone you give it to.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/grimfeld/synesis/issues.
