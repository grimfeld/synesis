# Paul in Ephesus

Ac 19 covers the three years in [[Ephesus]] (Ac 20:31). [[Paul]] taught daily in the school auditorium of Tyrannus (Ac 19:9, 10).

The riot in the theatre (Ac 19:23-41) is the backdrop for the farewell at Miletus, Ac 20:17-38.

Priscilla and Aquila were there earlier ([[Priscilla]], Ac 18:18, 19) and [[Timothy]] was left in charge (1Ti 1:3).
