# Undeserved kindness in Romans

Ro 3:24 declares people righteous "as a free gift". [[Paul]] builds on it in Ro 5:15-17: the gift is not like the trespass.

Eph 2:8 says the same from another angle. Compare [[Undeserved kindness]] as a concept across the letters, and how #undeserved-kindness relates to #endurance in Ro 5:3-5.
