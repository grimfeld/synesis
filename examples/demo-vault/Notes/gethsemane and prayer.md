# Gethsemane and prayer

Mt 26:36-46 and Lu 22:39-46. [[Jesus]] prayed three times in [[Gethsemane]], the same words each time (v. 44).

What strikes me: he asked for the disciples' company, then accepted being alone. Php 4:6, 7 promises the peace that came at the end of that prayer.
