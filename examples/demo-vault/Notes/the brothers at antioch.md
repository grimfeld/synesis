# The brothers at Antioch

Antioch is where the disciples were first called Christians (Ac 11:26). The
congregation there sent Barnabas and Saul out with relief for Judea, and later
sent them off on the first missionary journey (Ac 13:2, 3).

Barnabas' encouragement shows in how he went to Tarsus to look for Saul and
brought him back to teach for a whole year (Ac 11:25, 26).

Troas comes later in the account, but the pattern is the same: a congregation
that sends people out rather than keeping them.
