# Endurance in trials

Jas 1:2-4 says to consider it all joy. The testing of faith works out endurance (v. 3), and endurance has to finish its work (v. 4).

## Cross-references

- Ro 5:3-5 links tribulation, endurance, an approved condition, hope.
- Heb 12:1-3 puts endurance in a race, looking at [[Jesus]].
- [[Paul]] wrote about this from prison (Php 4:13).
