# Psalm 23 reflections

Ps 23 as a whole is a day with the shepherd. Ps 23:4 is the centre: the valley, the rod, the staff.

[[David]] wrote as a former shepherd (1Sa 16:11). Compare John 10:11-15 where [[Jesus]] takes the title.
